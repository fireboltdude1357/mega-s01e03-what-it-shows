What each one is

parser. The shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant in #4471 writes the day first. 2 March comes out as 3 February. Fix it in the parser: read dates in the tenant’s order.

hint. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads it day first. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants land here. The parser fix does not touch this. The hint has to match the tenant’s order.

one-day. Some bookings move by one day and keep their month. A day and month swap cannot do that. One meeting showed as Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto, and one invite arrived before its booking. That points at time zone conversion. The desk had already noted these reports in desk-notes.json, but the #4471 table still counts them under parser.

How the count works

  • The export pages overlap, so some reports come twice. Count each id once.
  • Place a report by its tenant: locale and booking form (locales.ts, causes.ts). Do not use the intake tag. Intake guesses it from the reporter’s browser, so every en-US browser got hint, whatever the tenant runs.
  • Read the body before you merge. A one-day move and a month flip share subject lines, so matching on subject (decision 0001) put them together.

The table above runs on the export at build time. When the desk merges more reports into #4471, the counts here follow.
